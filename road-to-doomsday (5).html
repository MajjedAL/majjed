How to watch the MCU before Avengers: Doomsday

The Marvel Cinematic Universe began with Iron Man in 2008 and grew into a large set of connected stories. Events in one film can change what happens in another, so new viewers often don’t know where to start.

You don’t need to watch everything. This guide follows the stories that give the most useful background, from the first Avengers team-up to the multiverse.

2008–2011

Start where the MCU began

Iron Man introduces Tony Stark, a wealthy inventor who builds a powerful suit of armor. He starts out caring mostly about his company and his own life. After a difficult experience, he decides to use his inventions to protect other people.

Tony becomes one of the central characters of the early MCU, and his choices shape many later events. The four films after it, found under Optional, each introduce a different hero and a new part of the Marvel world, which sets up the larger story.

2012–2015

Meet the Avengers

In The Avengers, Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye work together to protect Earth from an alien attack. They have different personalities and don’t always agree, but they find that working as a team gives them a better chance.

The team doesn’t succeed right away. The heroes have to learn to trust each other, which makes the story more than a battle between heroes and villains.

2016–2017

Why the Avengers separate

In Captain America: Civil War, the Avengers disagree about whether governments should control their actions. Tony Stark believes rules and supervision may prevent future disasters. Steve Rogers worries that outside control could stop heroes from helping people when they are needed most.

Both sides have good reasons for their choices, so the conflict is more than good against evil. It also explains why the heroes are not always united when new dangers appear. After it, Doctor Strange brings in magic and new ideas about reality, and Thor: Ragnarok expands the cosmic side of the MCU.

2018–2019

Follow the story of Thanos

These two films bring years of connected stories to a turning point. In Infinity War, Thanos searches for the six Infinity Stones, believing they will let him solve a major problem in the universe. The Avengers, the Guardians of the Galaxy, Spider-Man and Doctor Strange all try to stop him.

Endgame follows the surviving heroes as they deal with what happened and decide how to move forward. For anyone preparing for Doomsday, these are among the most useful films. They explain the history of the Avengers and show why the relationships between characters matter.

  • 2018
    Avengers: Infinity WarThanos searches for the six Infinity Stones.
    Must watch
  • 2019
    Avengers: EndgameThe survivors decide how to move forward.
    Must watch

2021–2022

Explore the multiverse

After Endgame, Marvel looks more closely at the multiverse: the idea that different universes can exist, sometimes with different versions of familiar characters. This lets stories reach beyond a single timeline.

Knowing about the multiverse does not mean knowing what Doomsday will do. Some details haven’t been revealed, and many online predictions are only fan theories.

2022–2025

Get to know the newer heroes

Marvel has kept adding characters and teams since the original Avengers story. Learning about a few of them shows how the MCU has changed.

Not every film here is equally necessary. With limited time, start with The Fantastic Four: First Steps and pick others based on the characters you want to understand.

Confirmed or rumored?

Fans like to predict who will become friends, who will fight and which surprises are coming. These ideas are fun to discuss, but treat them as fact only when Marvel or Disney announces them.

Final thoughts

Preparing for Avengers: Doomsday doesn’t mean watching every Marvel movie and show. Focus on the stories that introduce the main heroes, explain the history of the Avengers and explore the multiverse.

You don’t need to remember every small detail. Understanding the main story is a good way to enjoy the film and see why the different heroes are coming together.
